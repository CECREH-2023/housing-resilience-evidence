# Housing Resilience Evidence Library

This collection contains four thematic syntheses of housing-resilience research, covering measurement, longitudinal recovery, community transformation, and technology and infrastructure. The bibliographic index contains 92 records: 40 community-restoration, 22 technology/infrastructure, 19 measurement, and 11 longitudinal records. The reports retain their original literature-search windows; the September documentation review is not a new systematic literature search.

## Results and interpretation

Four thematic syntheses cover measurement, longitudinal recovery, community transformation, and technology and infrastructure. A central finding of the measurement synthesis is that housing resilience is commonly operationalized through **habitability, functionality, recovery time, and household trajectories**, rather than structural safety alone.

The reports distinguish building, household, and community scales and emphasize that indicators have different data requirements and validity limits. This is a curated evidence resource, not a new meta-analysis. Use the bibliographic index to assess the original studies; every literature claim has not been re-audited for this distribution.
